Pofuland Uploader

Pofuland Uploader is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the Pofuland YouTube channels to upload their own original videos. It uses YouTube API Services.

  • Uploads the owner's own finished videos to the owner's own two channels.
  • Sets the title, description, tags, thumbnail and language.
  • Always marks every video as Made for Kids.

It is not a public app: it has no other users, is not offered to anyone else and collects no data. See our Privacy Policy.
